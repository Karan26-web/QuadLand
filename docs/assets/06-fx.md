# 06 · Effects sprites

Particles in the game are small sprites drawn hundreds at a time: spray when zones rise, mist under a landing ship, sparks and smoke when a ship crashes, cloud puffs on the way down to the sea. All are **BLACK (sprite)** deliveries. The installer turns brightness into opacity and the colour white, and the game tints each particle itself, so paint them in white and greys only.

| Slot | Size | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| `cloud` | 1024 × 1024 | The cloud deck the command ship drops through (Screen 2); about 70 big puffs, tinted violet |
| `smoke` | 512 × 512 | Mist pushed out from under a touchdown; a crashed ship's smoke; mist where islands rise |
| `splash` | 512 × 512 | Spray and droplets: islands breaking the surface, water pouring off them, a ship splashing into the sea |
| `spark` | 512 × 128 | Sparks when a ship hits an oversized zone |

```
cloud: One soft, fluffy cumulus cloud puff, white and pale grey, round and billowy with a flatter base, soft wispy edges that fade completely to black well inside the frame. Centred. Nothing else.
smoke: One soft round puff of smoke, white and pale grey, wispy and translucent at its edges, fading completely to black well inside the frame. Centred. Nothing else.
splash: A burst of water spray seen from slightly above: a ring of droplets and small splashes flying outward from the centre, the biggest drops near the middle, fine mist at the rim, in white on black, fading out well inside the frame. Nothing else.
spark: One horizontal streak of a hot spark: a bright white core about a third of the way from the right end, tapering to thin glowing tails at both ends, in white on black. Centred vertically. Nothing else.
```
**Accept if:** the element is white or grey only; it fades to black well before the edges (a hard edge shows as a square when hundreds of sprites overlap); it is centred.

These are optional: the procedural sprites already look right. Make them last.
