# Glossary: words that must stay the same everywhere

Use the project's own glossary first (a learning module, the textbook the learners use). These tables come from the
ConveGenius *spacecraft landing mission learning* module (`learning/locales/locales-quadrilaterals.json` in the
Spaceship Mission repo) and the game that was localized with it. They are a starting point, not a review: a native
speaker who teaches the subject should check anything new.

## Register

The polite form, as the learning module uses it, for instructions to a child:

| | Hindi | Marathi | Gujarati | Telugu | Odia |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| you | आप | तुम्ही | તમે | మీరు | ଆପଣ |
| press | दबाएँ | दाबा | દબાવો | నొక్కండి | ଦବାନ୍ତୁ |
| choose | चुनें | निवडा | પસંદ કરો | ఎంచుకోండి | ବାଛନ୍ତୁ |
| let's … | चलिए, … | चला, … | ચાલો, … | రండి, … | ଚାଲନ୍ତୁ, … |

| units | इकाई | एकक | એકમ | యూనిట్లు | ଏକକ |

Telugu rarely reads "×" as a word; say the action instead: భుజాన్ని భుజంతో గుణించాలి ("multiply side by side").
Formulas on screen can keep their symbols: `½ × d₁ × d₂` and `(a + b)` stay in Latin letters in every language,
as the learning module writes them.

## Number words

For the voice tool (`voice-lab/textnorm.py`, `NUMBERS`), which says digits as words. These are the numbers Spaceship
Mission needed, plus 0-20 and the tens. Add what your game says, and have a speaker check each new one: Hindi,
Marathi, Gujarati and Odia number words from 21 to 99 are irregular, so never generate them by rule.

| | Hindi | Marathi | Gujarati | Telugu | Odia |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | चार | चार | ચાર | నాలుగు | ଚାରି |
| 5 | पाँच | पाच | પાંચ | ఐదు | ପାଞ୍ଚ |
| 8 | आठ | आठ | આઠ | ఎనిమిది | ଆଠ |
| 12 | बारह | बारा | બાર | పన్నెండు | ବାର |
| 20 | बीस | वीस | વીસ | ఇరవై | କୋଡ଼ିଏ |
| 24 | चौबीस | चोवीस | ચોવીસ | ఇరవై నాలుగు | ଚବିଶି |
| 40 | चालीस | चाळीस | ચાળીસ | నలభై | ଚାଳିଶି |
| 42 | बयालीस | बेचाळीस | બેતાળીસ | నలభై రెండు | ବୟାଳିଶି |
| 60 | साठ | साठ | સાઠ | అరవై | ଷାଠିଏ |
| 100 | सौ | शंभर | સો | వంద | ଶହେ |

The full tables are in `scripts/voice-lab/textnorm.py`.

Case endings attach to the word the number becomes: in Telugu `5ని 5తో` is said ఐదుని ఐదుతో, so write the
suffix straight after the digit in the strings and the conversion keeps it.
