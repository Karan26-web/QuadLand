Voice Lab

The game's Ship AI in Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, Telugu and Odia, spoken by open speech models running on a Mac. No cloud API and no per-word bill: pick a line, listen down the voices, and star the one you want for each language.

Clarity: how much of the line a speech recogniser heard exactly as written. Pitch: the voice's measured fundamental.

Your picks

    Picks stay in this browser. Paste the config into assets/vo/voices.json and run python3 tools/make_vo.py --lang all to voice the whole game.
